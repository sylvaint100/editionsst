01 — The book

This book isn't about technology. It's about decisions: which task to automate, how to build a workflow that holds, how to design checking before a well-worded error slips past unnoticed. No product named, no screenshots — only grids, thresholds, and numbers nobody else publishes.

For small business owners, managers, and consultants ready to move from the why to the how.

Decisions

Which task to hand to the machine, and where to stop. A grid, not a hunch.

Workflow

How to chain the steps so the result still holds next week, not just today.

Checking

Designed before the error, because a wrong answer that reads well does not announce itself.

03 — The author

Sylvain Tremblay has been an IT consultant and entrepreneur for more than thirty years. He has founded and run several businesses, which gave him firsthand knowledge of what small businesses actually work under: tight budgets, small teams, decisions made without a safety net.

Since the arrival of generative AI, he's spent a good part of his time testing it in his own businesses rather than reading about its promises. His books come from that practice, not from theory.

He is the author of AI in Action. The Gap Is You. is the second book in the series.
