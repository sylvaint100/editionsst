The Gap Is You.

Decide · Build · Verify · Last — with AI.

Everyone now has the same AI tools. That raises the floor — not the ceiling. The gap between the two is you.

This book isn't about technology. It's about decisions: which task to automate, how to build a workflow that holds, how to design checking before a well-worded error slips past unnoticed. No product named, no screenshots — only grids, thresholds, and numbers nobody else publishes.

For small business owners, managers, and consultants ready to move from the why to the how.

03 — The imprint

A small house, books that earn their keep

ST Publishing is the English-language imprint of Éditions ST, a Québec publishing house founded by Sylvain Tremblay, a strategy and artificial intelligence consultant. The house publishes his own books along with illustrated titles and puzzle collections.

Every title carries an ISBN assigned by Bibliothèque et Archives nationales du Québec and is legally deposited. Books are printed on demand and shipped from the printer nearest the reader.

The French catalogue is at editionsst.com.
