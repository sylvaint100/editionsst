Privacy policy

This page explains what data Éditions ST collects on editionsst.com, why, and how you can manage it. It applies to both versions of the site — its French version, and this English one.

Last updated — September 1, 2026

01 — Who is responsible

Éditions ST is a Québec publishing house founded by Sylvain Tremblay. It decides the purposes and means of the processing described here, for editionsst.com and its English-language imprint, ST Publishing (editionsst.com/en/).

The domain stpublishing.ca is an alternate address for this page: it redirects to editionsst.com/en. This policy applies there as well.

The point of contact for any question or request about your personal information is info@editionsst.com.

The person responsible for the protection of personal information, as required under Québec's Law 25, is Sylvain Tremblay, reachable at the same address.

02 — What data, and why

Meta (Facebook) pixel

Used only if you click "Accept" in the consent banner, to measure the site's audience and show targeted ads on Meta's platforms. Until you agree, no data is shared with Meta. Legal basis: your consent.

Your consent choice

Whether you accept or decline, that choice is stored only in your browser's local storage — never on a server — so you aren't asked again on every visit. This technical information is strictly necessary for the banner to work and is not itself subject to consent.

Fonts (Google Fonts)

The Spectral and IBM Plex Mono fonts used on the site are served from Google's servers, which shares your IP address with Google when the page loads. Legal basis: legitimate interest in displaying the site.

Embedded video (HeyGen)

On the French page for L'écart, c'est vous. only, a presentation video is embedded from HeyGen's servers. Loading it shares technical information with that service, under its own policy. Legal basis: legitimate interest in that page's functioning.

Email correspondence

The site has no forms: contact happens through an email link. If you write to us, we receive your address and the content of your message, used only to reply.

Hosting logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc. / Microsoft), which keeps standard technical logs, including IP addresses, as part of its own service. Éditions ST has no access to those logs beyond what GitHub makes available.

The site has no user accounts, no newsletter, no data-collecting forms, and no audience-measurement tool other than the Meta pixel described above.

03 — Who this data is shared with
  • Meta Platforms, Inc. — measurement and advertising pixel, only after your consent.
  • Google LLC — font delivery.
  • HeyGen — hosting of the embedded video, French page for L'écart, c'est vous. only.
  • GitHub, Inc. (Microsoft) — site hosting.

Meta is a US company: data you share with it by accepting the pixel is therefore transferred outside Québec and the European Union, to the United States, under Meta's own retention and protection rules. No information is sold; Éditions ST does not share data for any purpose beyond what is described here.

04 — How long data is kept
  • Meta pixel — under Meta's own retention periods, outside Éditions ST's direct control.
  • Consent choice — kept in your browser until you clear it or change your choice.
  • Emails received — kept as long as needed to handle your request.
05 — Your rights

At any time, you can:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • have it corrected if it is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • request that it be deleted;
  • withdraw your consent to the Meta pixel;
  • request its portability, where the law provides for it.

To exercise these rights, write to info@editionsst.com.

If you believe your rights are not being respected, you may file a complaint with Québec's Commission d'accès à l'information or, if the GDPR applies to your situation, with the data protection authority of your country of residence in the European Union.

06 — Withdrawing your consent

The "Cookies" button at the bottom of every page reopens the consent banner, where you can change your choice at any time. You can also clear your browser's local storage for this site, which has the same effect.

07 — Changes to this policy

This page may be updated if the processing described here changes — for example, a new tool or a new third party. The date at the top of the page shows the last revision.
