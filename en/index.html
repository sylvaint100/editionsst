03 — The imprint

A small house, books that earn their keep

ST Publishing is the English-language imprint of Éditions ST, a Québec publishing house founded by Sylvain Tremblay, a strategy and artificial intelligence consultant. The house publishes his own books along with illustrated titles and puzzle collections.

Every title carries an ISBN assigned by Bibliothèque et Archives nationales du Québec and is legally deposited. Books are printed on demand and shipped from the printer nearest the reader.

The French catalogue is at editionsst.com.
